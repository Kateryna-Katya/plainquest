How to Play

Simple to start. Impossible to master.

Not all treasure is gold. The Bay rewards patience, skill, and a little recklessness.

  • 01

    Choose Your Zone

    Pick from seven bay regions, each with different fish density, treasure rarity, and danger level. Beginners start at Pelican Cove. Legends head to the Abyss Trench.

  • 02

    Fish or Hunt

    Switch between fishing mode and treasure dive mode at any time. Fish to earn coin and crafting materials. Dive to find legendary loot and rare map fragments.

  • 03

    Upgrade Your Gear

    Spend your haul on better rods, deeper-dive suits, faster boats, and precision sonar. Better gear unlocks access to restricted bay zones.

  • 04

    Compete or Explore

    Enter high-stakes tournaments for regional dominance or chart unmarked wrecks for massive solo rewards.
